Review status

These are preparation notes for Kasapa’s Milestone 3 prototype, not a statement of App Store submission, approval, or compliance certification. Distribution readiness must be assessed against the actual submitted build.

Current behavior

The reviewed app is a native SwiftUI prototype with local JSON persistence, no network backend, no account server, and no third-party packages. Profile sharing, messaging, block/report, and action history operate locally.

Suggested review flow

Complete local onboarding; inspect Discover; send and accept an eligible incoming connection; change an exact field/card grant; open a connected conversation; delete a thread; block and unblock a peer. Confirm that unblocking restores neither grants nor the former connection.

Safety and messaging boundaries

Local reports do not reach a human moderation service. Messages have no remote delivery or read receipts. A production social service would need separate assessment of moderation, abuse handling, account lifecycle, and data disclosures before release.

Before submission

Check the release build’s permissions, bundled SDKs, privacy disclosures, screenshots, support URLs, and any account/deletion flows. Review these notes whenever networking or third-party services are added; do not reuse the local-only description for a changed architecture.

Review contact

For build-specific questions contact support@edentv.us. State the build number and review step. This public page contains no reviewer credentials.
