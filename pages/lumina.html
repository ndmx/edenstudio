Lumina is a design system for tokens, themes, layout decisions, and feedback. This interactive study explores how balance, contrast, rhythm, and unity change through color, geometry, and motion.

Choose a principle, then compare the three visual styles. The scene uses a lighter rendering mode on small screens and responds to your device’s reduced-motion preference.

What this study explores

The experiment separates a shared composition from its visual treatment. Atelier uses warm light and soft surfaces; Memphis adds playful color; Cyber-Brutalist emphasizes hard edges and contrast.

The aim is to learn where expressive design supports an experience and where it distracts. This showcase lives in the studio so the personal portfolio can stay focused on work, experience, and outcomes.
